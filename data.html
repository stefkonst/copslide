How the numbers are made

  1. Orientation. Gyro bias and gravity come from the still second before each recording triggers; the gyro is integrated to an orientation with a weak tilt correction that only acts when the board is barely moving. The rotation about the vertical gives the heading — its changes are exact (no magnetometer needed).
  2. Finding the descent. A recording also holds getting in, scooting and climbing out. The unmistakable part is the turn pair: a right turn (heading falls 100–155°) directly followed by a left turn. The 10 % and 90 % points of each turn are pinned to the slide (right turn = 2.35 m, left = 1.00 m).
  3. Speed. Distance between landmarks ÷ time between them. A slide model (dv/dt = g sin θ − μ·N − drag, wall reaction N from gravity + centripetal load in quadrature) is fitted through the landmarks: its effective friction μ_eff and entry speed then give the full speed profile and the 3D trajectory.
  4. Checks. v = acentripetal/yaw rate in the right turn (no geometry needed) reads ~2× higher — it is biased upward by bumps and tilt error, so it is shown only as an upper bound. A sweep of the unknown decline angle (15–25°) shows where the riders stall is robust; the absolute μ is not.
Caveats. One run per rider per condition — differences between suit and normal clothes are indicative, not statistically tested. The decline angle (20°) and turn sweeps are estimates, not a survey. Run #18's identity is flagged “?” in its name and its turn pair is the weakest of the set; Angel's normal-clothes run (#15) never reached the turns while recording. Run #16 is not a descent.

Recording and syncing

  1. Wear the logger at the waist. Triple-tap (or tap Arm on the phone page) and hold still ~1 s → READY. It triggers on the push-off and keeps ~2.2 s from before; lie still 2.5 s at the bottom to auto-stop (or Stop on the phone).
  2. On the phone: join Wi-Fi CopSlide → http://192.168.4.1. Rename runs, view raw plots, Analyze for an on-phone speed estimate and 3D replay, download BIN/CSV.
  3. Full analysis on a computer — USB sync and the pipeline (writes results/ and this page's data): 
    python tools/serial_sync.py COM3 data/raw/logger
