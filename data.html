How the numbers are made

  1. Orientation. Gyro bias and gravity come from the still second before each recording triggers; the gyro is integrated to an orientation with a weak tilt correction that only acts when the board is barely moving. The rotation about the vertical gives the heading — its changes are exact (no magnetometer needed).
  2. Finding the descent. A recording also holds getting in, scooting and climbing out. The unmistakable part is the turn pair: a right turn (heading falls 100–155°) directly followed by a left turn. The 10 % and 90 % points of each turn are pinned to the slide (right turn = 2.35 m, left = 1.00 m).
  3. Reaching the bottom. Every recording ran until the rider was out, so the end of the ride is a landmark too: the first moment after the turns the rider is lying still at the bottom (no rubbing vibration, no rotation), or gets up, whichever is first.
  4. Speed. Distance between landmarks ÷ time between them. A slide model (dv/dt = g sin θ − μ·N − drag, wall reaction N from gravity + centripetal load in quadrature) is fitted in two zones: friction on the upper slope and turns from the four turn landmarks, friction on the lower slide so the rider comes to rest on the exit flat when the IMU says they did. Run backwards to the top, it also gives the push-off speed (~1.9 m/s on average) — the simulation presets are started with that same push.
  5. Checks. v = acentripetal/yaw rate in the right turn (no geometry needed) reads ~2× higher — it is biased upward by bumps and tilt error, so it is shown only as an upper bound. A sweep of the unknown slope angle (15–25°) shows the timing is robust; the absolute μ is not.
Caveats. One run per rider per condition — differences between suit and normal clothes are indicative, not statistically tested, and normal clothes always went first. The slope (20°) and turn angles are estimates, not a survey. Stef's normal-clothes run (#18) is uncertain: its name has a “?”, and its turn landmarks don't fit one smooth ride (1.4 s between the turns vs. ~0.4 s in every other run). Angel's normal-clothes run (#15) never reached the turns while recording. Run #16 is not a descent; Rider A (#13) is left out.

Recording and syncing

  1. Wear the logger at the waist. Triple-tap (or tap Arm on the phone page) and hold still ~1 s → READY. It triggers on the push-off and keeps ~2.2 s from before; lie still 2.5 s at the bottom to auto-stop (or Stop on the phone).
  2. On the phone: join Wi-Fi CopSlide → http://192.168.4.1. Rename runs, view raw plots, Analyze for an on-phone speed estimate and 3D replay, download BIN/CSV.
  3. Full analysis on a computer — USB sync and the pipeline (writes results/ and this page's data): 
    python tools/serial_sync.py COM3 data/raw/logger
