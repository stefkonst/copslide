how did the cop go so fast?
SimulationData
  1. Everyone was far slower than the cop. Through the turns our 19 riders did 1–5 km/h (walking pace); the cop did at least 6 km/h there and most likely 13–17.
  2. Speed in the turns decides how the ride ends: 2 km/h → stopped before the flat, 3 km/h → stopped on it, 4 km/h → shot off the end (p = 0.006).
  3. The riders who shot off were about as slippery as the cop on the lower straight (friction 0.20, range 0.13–0.26, vs his 0.19–0.29), and reached up to 20 km/h there vs his 22. The gap is in the turns, where everyone sat at the stall point.
  4. Cop suit, sex, age, drinks, weight, height and contact area made no clear difference. Jeans were slowest (suggestive, p = 0.02; not conclusive across 8 comparisons).
  5. It felt faster than it was: every rider who shot off rated themselves closer to the cop than they were (on average "7 out of 10" when they were at 5; p = 0.008), but riders ranked themselves in the right order.
