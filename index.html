Effect of each parameter

Each figure changes one choice and keeps the others at our riders' values; computed with the same physics.

Push-off speed

Expected: A harder push helps: going from 2 to 16 km/h cuts our riders' time from 5.9 to 3.2 s. But even a 16 km/h push with our friction is slower than the cop with a gentle push (2.2–2.9 s). Push helps; it does not close the gap.

Starting position

Expected: With our riders' friction the position changes the time by about ±15 % (sitting up 4.2 s, cop position 5.7 s). With the cop's friction every position takes about 2.65 s. Position mostly changes how much the body twists (76–176°).

Clothes (= fitted friction)

Expected: Same push for all: the curves split in the turns (ours drop to about 5 km/h, the cop keeps 13–15) and the gap never closes. Clothes friction in the turns decides the run.

Physics: Rapier rigid-body engine; slide geometry from the measured segment lengths (turn angles estimated from the gyro); friction per clothes setting fitted to the logger + video of those runs. Details on the Landing simulation page and in Results.
